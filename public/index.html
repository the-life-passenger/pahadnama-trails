05 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Got questions before heading into the mountains? We've got you covered.

Yes, absolutely! Over 40% of our participants are solo travellers, including many first-time solo women. Our trek leaders ensure a respectful, safe, and supportive environment throughout the journey from pickup to drop-off.

Night-departure treks (like Harishchandragad and Kalsubai) depart late Friday/Saturday night from Mumbai, reach the base by dawn, complete the trek during the day, and return to Mumbai by Sunday evening. Early-morning treks (like Devkund) depart early morning (05:00 AM) and return the same night.

Proper trekking shoes with deep rubber lug soles (such as Quechua/Wildcraft/Action trekking) are mandatory. Avoid flat sneakers, canvas shoes, sandals, or formal shoes as wet Sahyadri rock patches can be slippery.

Cancellations requested 48 hours prior to the batch departure are eligible for an 80% refund or free rollover to any upcoming weekend batch. Cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable as private transport and village meals are pre-booked.

After transferring the payment via UPI or filling the Google Form, send a screenshot of the payment along with your full name and chosen trek date to our WhatsApp coordinator at +91 91377 61400. You will receive a booking confirmation voucher and WhatsApp group link.
